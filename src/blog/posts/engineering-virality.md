Every brand wants the viral moment. Very few build the machine that produces them.

After scaling brands across Instagram, YouTube and X, the pattern is clear: the accounts that "get lucky" repeatedly aren't lucky at all. They run a system.

## 1. Hooks are the product

The first two seconds decide whether anything else you made gets seen. We treat hooks as their own creative asset — written, tested and iterated separately from the body of the content.

- Lead with the outcome, not the setup
- Open a loop the viewer needs closed
- Say something the audience already believes, in a way they've never heard

## 2. Test in volume, scale in focus

Algorithms reward consistency, but they reveal winners through volume. We publish more variations than feels comfortable, watch retention curves, and then pour distribution behind the top 10%.

> Your audience tells you what works. Most brands just aren't publishing enough to hear them.

## 3. Package for the platform

The same idea needs a different wrapper on Reels, Shorts and LinkedIn. Aspect ratio, pacing, caption length and even the first frame should change per platform.

## 4. Build the loop

A post that goes viral once is a spike. A post that feeds followers into a newsletter, a community or a product is a system. Always design where the attention goes next.

---

Virality isn't a lottery ticket. It's an engineering problem — and engineering problems can be solved.
