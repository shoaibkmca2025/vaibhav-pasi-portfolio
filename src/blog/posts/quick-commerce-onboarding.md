Quick commerce has rewritten how Indian consumers buy. For emerging brands, getting onto these platforms is one challenge — winning once you're there is another.

## Get compliance right first

Most delays we see come from paperwork, not product. Before applying:

1. Keep GST, FSSAI (if applicable) and trademark documents ready
2. Standardise product dimensions, weights and barcodes
3. Prepare MRP-compliant packaging images

## Your catalog is your storefront

On a 10-minute delivery app, nobody reads long descriptions. The **thumbnail, title and price** do all the selling.

- Titles should lead with the brand and the key benefit
- Images need to read clearly at thumbnail size
- Pack sizes should match how people actually buy on the platform

## Price for the basket, not the product

Quick-commerce shoppers build baskets. Entry-level pack sizes and combo offers often outperform your hero SKU at launch.

> Launch small, learn fast, then expand your range based on what actually sells in each city.

## Measure what matters

Track availability, search rank and conversion by dark store — not just overall sales. A product that's out of stock in your best-performing area is revenue you'll never see.
