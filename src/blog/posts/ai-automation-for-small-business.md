AI adoption isn't about replacing your team. It's about giving them back the hours they lose to repetitive work.

## 1. Lead capture and qualification

Connect your website forms and WhatsApp to a CRM, and let an AI assistant ask the first few qualifying questions. Your sales team only talks to people ready to buy.

## 2. Content repurposing

Turn one long-form video into captions, short clips, a blog post and an email. One recording, a week of content.

## 3. Customer support triage

Most support questions repeat. An AI layer can answer the common ones instantly and route the rest to the right person with context attached.

## 4. Reporting on autopilot

Pull ads, sales and social data into a single weekly summary written in plain language. Decisions get faster when the numbers come to you.

## 5. Catalog and listing updates

For marketplace sellers, AI can draft product titles, descriptions and keyword variations in bulk — reviewed by a human, published in minutes.

> Start with the task your team complains about most. That's usually your highest-ROI automation.
