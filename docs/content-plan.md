## Briefs

### 3. Website SEO Audit Checklist for Business Owners
- **Audience:** owners of small business websites who suspect they "aren't on Google".
- **Intent:** informational, close to commercial (they may want the audit done for them).
- **Questions to answer:** Is my site indexed? Why don't I rank for my own business name? What do title tags and descriptions do? Is my site fast enough on mobile? Do I need structured data?
- **Outline:** Answer first (a checklist in 10 steps) → indexing (Search Console coverage, `site:` search) → titles and descriptions → headings and content → speed and Core Web Vitals → mobile → structured data → internal links → Google Business Profile → what to do next.
- **Internal links:** `/services/seo`, `/blog/what-is-answer-engine-optimization`, `/contact#enquire`.
- **Evidence needed:** none beyond public documentation (Google Search Central). Screenshots of Search Console from this site would help.

### 4. How to Build a Website That Generates Qualified Leads
- **Audience:** businesses with a website that gets visits but few enquiries.
- **Intent:** informational.
- **Questions:** What makes a visitor enquire? Where should forms and WhatsApp buttons go? How many fields should a form have? How do I know which pages produce leads?
- **Outline:** Answer first → one goal per page → message clarity above the fold → proof near the CTA → form design → WhatsApp and call options → speed → tracking `generate_lead` in GA4 → review cycle.
- **Internal links:** `/services/website-development`, `/work`, `/blog/website-development-cost-in-india`.
- **Evidence needed:** optional before/after enquiry numbers from a client site, with permission.

### 5. How to Measure the Results of Digital Marketing Campaigns
- **Audience:** business owners paying for marketing and unsure whether it works.
- **Intent:** informational.
- **Questions:** Which metrics matter (and which are vanity)? How do I connect social views to enquiries? What should a monthly report contain?
- **Outline:** Answer first → reach vs engagement vs conversion metrics → UTM links → GA4 events → CRM or sheet for enquiries → monthly report template → how to read the client results on `/client-wins` (views are not sales).
- **Internal links:** `/services/digital-marketing`, `/client-wins`, `/contact#enquire`.
- **Evidence needed:** none; can reference the published case studies as examples of metric definitions.

## Rules for every article
- Open with a direct answer in the first one or two sentences.
- Descriptive, question-style headings; one topic per article.
- No invented statistics, clients, quotes or results. Attribute any external figure with a link.
- Never promise rankings or AI citations.
- Cover image: real project media where possible; decorative stock is acceptable but must not be presented as client work.
- After publishing: check the article appears in `/sitemap.xml` and request indexing in Search Console.
